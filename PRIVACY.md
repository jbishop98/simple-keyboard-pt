We don't collect or send your data. Period. The app has no internet permission.

Word suggestions are off by default. If you turn them on together with "Learn from typing",
the words you type (and which word followed which) are stored only on your device, in the
app's private storage. They are excluded from Android cloud backups and device transfers,
never leave the device, and can be deleted at any time in Settings > Word suggestions >
Clear learned words. Nothing is learned from password fields or from apps that ask the
keyboard not to learn (such as incognito browser tabs).
